# Business Analysis Registers

Product OS keeps analysis records distinct because they answer different management questions.

- **Decisions** capture the question, options, rationale, outcome and decision state.
- **Risks** capture uncertain future events, causes, consequences, mitigation and contingency.
- **Dependencies** capture something outside the immediate work that must be available or completed.
- **Stakeholders** capture people or groups affected by the work, their role, interest, influence and engagement approach.
- **Issues** capture events or conditions that have already occurred and require investigation or resolution.
- **Change requests** capture proposed changes and their scope, schedule, cost and risk implications, with a recorded review outcome.

All six record types are scoped to an organisation and product. They may optionally link to an initiative in the same product and organisation. Create and update operations write audit records. They are not interchangeable with action items: an action item can be created to carry out a mitigation, investigate an issue, engage a stakeholder or implement an approved change.

## Stakeholder influence and engagement

Influence is rated from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Engagement uses UNAWARE, RESISTANT, NEUTRAL, SUPPORTIVE and LEADING. These are working analysis labels, not automatic judgements; users remain responsible for assessing and updating them.

## Issues versus risks

Use a risk when the event may happen. Use an issue when the event has happened or the condition currently exists. A risk can materialise into an issue, but the records remain separate so the original exposure and the actual response can both be understood.

## Change control

A change request should state why the change is needed and assess scope, schedule, cost and risk impacts before a decision is recorded. Approval does not itself create or complete delivery tasks; implementation work should be planned and tracked through the existing initiative and action-item capabilities.
